About the shop

A shop where you deal with the owner himself.

I run this shop right here in the Suriyawan–Bipash area. Most of my customers are people from nearby villages and lanes, and many of them walk in with a simple question: "Which phone is good for my money?" I like answering that honestly.

I would rather sell you the right phone at a fair price than the costliest one. If a cover does not fit or a charger does not suit your phone, tell me and we will sort it out.

Need a recharge late in the evening? We are open till 10 PM, every day of the week.
